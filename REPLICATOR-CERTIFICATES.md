# REPL — Replicator Certificate Archive

**Research-ID:** REPL  
**Author:** Philippe Beauchamp  
**AI assistance:** ChatGPT  
**Status:** Execution/reproducibility track; certificate contents to be populated from verified replicator work.

## Purpose

This archive is reserved for the actual replicator/execution technology and its reproducibility certificates.

It is intentionally separated from:

- HODGE mathematical research;
- P–PN mathematical research.

## Certificate policy

Every REPL certificate must identify:

1. governing specification or theorem, when mathematical;
2. exact input;
3. exact execution procedure;
4. expected output;
5. independent reproduction method;
6. limitations and failure conditions.

## Boundary

No Hodge or P/PN claim is automatically transferred into this archive. A cross-track result must be explicitly identified and independently verified.

No proprietary keys, credentials, or operational secrets belong in this public file.
