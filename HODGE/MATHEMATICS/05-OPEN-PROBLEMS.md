# Hodge — Open Problems and Proof Obligations

1. **Geometric identification:** supply and prove the map from the candidate lattice to the intended ambient integral cohomology; do not reconstruct missing basis data by guesswork.
2. **Saturation:** prove saturation or calculate the exact index. For \(R=(P+Q)/2\), verify integrality and membership in the actual ambient lattice.
3. **Polarization/Rosati:** complete model-specific compatibility and positivity checks.
4. **Discriminant form:** state the lattice parity and quadratic-form convention, then verify the form and any gluing argument.
5. **Conjecture boundary:** the explicit model and its certificates are not a general proof of the Hodge Conjecture.

Mark each item **PROVED**, **DERIVED**, **COMPUTATIONALLY VERIFIED**, or **OPEN**. Arithmetic can be complete while its geometric interpretation remains open. Existing project certificate statuses take precedence over this summary.
