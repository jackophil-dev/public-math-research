# Hodge — Theorems and Proofs

## Proposition 1 — Discriminant order
For a non-degenerate integral Gram matrix \(G\), \(|L^*/L|=|\det G|\).

**Proof.** In a basis, the dual lattice is controlled by \(G^{-1}\); the index between the dual and original lattice is the absolute determinant of the integral pairing matrix. Hence the quotient has order \(|\det G|\). \(\square\)

## Proposition 2 — Cokernel from SNF
If the SNF is \(\operatorname{diag}(12,12)\), its cokernel is \((\mathbb Z/12)^2\).

**Proof.** Unimodular row/column operations preserve the cokernel up to isomorphism. The diagonal matrix's cokernel is the direct sum of the coordinate quotients. \(\square\)

## Limitation
These propositions prove abstract lattice facts conditional on the stated matrix/SNF. They do not establish that the lattice exhausts geometric algebraic-cycle classes on a particular fourfold. Consult [core theorems](../CORE-THEOREMS-01-04.md) and H01–H12 for project-specific assumptions and statuses. No general Hodge Conjecture proof is claimed.
