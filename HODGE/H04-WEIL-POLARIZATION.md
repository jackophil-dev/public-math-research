## 1. Certificate ID

**CERT-H04 — Weil-Type Polarization Symmetry**

## 2. Theorem Statement

A Weil-type abelian variety equipped with a polarization and an imaginary-quadratic CM action carries compatibility conditions between the polarization, the CM action, and the associated Rosati involution.

The research target is to express and verify those conditions in the explicit (K=mathbf Q(i)) model used by this program.

## 3. Definitions / Hypotheses

The intended calculation requires:

- the explicit abelian variety and polarization;
- the (K=mathbf Q(i)) action;
- the associated Rosati involution;
- the relevant trace/norm normalization;
- an explicitly chosen integral basis.

These data must be fixed before a model-specific numerical identity is promoted to a theorem.

## 4. Calculation / Proof

The current public record establishes the explicit Q(i) Weil calibration and its endomorphism-action formulas, but it does not contain a complete independent Rosati trace/norm verification sufficient to certify the full statement above.

Accordingly, this file records the precise theorem target rather than manufacturing missing polarization calculations.

## 5. Reproducibility Checkpoint

A completed verification must record:

1. the polarization matrix;
2. the CM action matrix;
3. the Rosati involution;
4. the trace/norm identities;
5. positivity compatibility;
6. the resulting invariant identities.
