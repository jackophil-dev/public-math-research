# P/PN — Mathematical Overview

**Public scope:** Part I, exterior arithmetic and primitive bivectors. [Zenodo record](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23196136).

For \(L_6\cong\mathbb Z^6\),
\[
\operatorname{rank}(\Lambda^2L_6)=\binom62=15.
\]
The alternating projection is \(\pi(x\otimes y)=x\wedge y\). The public dossier records \(\pi(P)=0\), \(\pi(Q)=2v\), and \(\pi(R)=v\), with the displayed coordinate vector for \(v\) primitive.

Separate the arithmetic block (integer relations, coordinates, SNF) from the geometric identification block. The map \(M_\iota:\mathbb Z^6\to H^1(E^4,\mathbb Z)\simeq\mathbb Z^8\) is not supplied and remains **OPEN**.
