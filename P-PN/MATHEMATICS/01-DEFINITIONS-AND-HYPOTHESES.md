# P/PN — Definitions and Hypotheses

Let \(L_6\cong\mathbb Z^6\) with ordered basis \(e_1,\ldots,e_6\). Its second exterior power has basis \(e_i\wedge e_j\), \(i<j\), and rank \(\binom62=15\). The exterior product is bilinear and alternating: \(x\wedge x=0\), \(x\wedge y=-y\wedge x\).

The universal alternating map is
\[
\pi:L_6\otimes L_6\to\Lambda^2L_6,\quad x\otimes y\mapsto x\wedge y.
\]
The dossier records \(a=b\), \(c=2d-a\), \(P=a\otimes b\), \(Q=c\otimes b\), and \(R=(P+Q)/2=d\otimes b\). The half-sum must be interpreted in its actual module; division by two is not automatically integral in an arbitrary tensor lattice.

A vector in \(\mathbb Z^n\) is primitive when its coordinate gcd is 1. SNF records integer-matrix cokernel structure, not geometric meaning. The cohomological map \(M_\iota\) is a separate missing datum, not an assumed identity.
