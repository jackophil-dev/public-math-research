# P/PN — Theorems and Proofs

## Proposition 1 — Rank of the exterior square
If \(L\) is free of rank \(n\), then \(\Lambda^2L\) has rank \(\binom n2\).

**Proof.** For a basis \(e_1,\ldots,e_n\), the elements \(e_i\wedge e_j\), \(i<j\), form a basis. There are \(n(n-1)/2\) such pairs; for \(n=6\), there are 15. \(\square\)

## Proposition 2 — Alternating projection
For every \(a\in L\), \(\pi(a\otimes a)=0\).

**Proof.** By definition this is \(a\wedge a\), which vanishes by alternation. \(\square\)

## Proposition 3 — Recorded vector is primitive
The dossier's vector has a coordinate equal to 1, hence its coordinate gcd is 1 and it is primitive.

## Limitation
These statements do not establish a geometric embedding into \(H^1(E^4,\mathbb Z)\). The map \(M_\iota\) and its provenance remain open proof obligations.
