# P/PN — Public Mathematical Research

This directory contains the public P/PN mathematical material corresponding to the P/PN Part I research package.

## Structure

- `CERTIFICATES/` — explicit computational and arithmetic verification certificates.
- `THEOREMS/` — theorem-level statements extracted from the verified P/PN arithmetic core.

## Scope

Part I concerns the integral exterior arithmetic of
\[
L_6\simeq\mathbb Z^6,\qquad \Lambda^2L_6.
\]

The geometric identification
\[
M_\iota:\mathbb Z^6\hookrightarrow H^1(E^4,\mathbb Z)\simeq\mathbb Z^8
\]
is **not** supplied here as a reconstructed datum. Its source remains an open provenance/identification gate.

Règle Zéro: no missing geometric data are invented.
