## Governing objects

Current working discrete construction:
\[
P_N=\prod_{i=1}^{N}\Phi\!\left(\frac{\Delta_i}{\sigma}\right),
\]
where
\[
\Delta_i=\operatorname{dist}(I(t_i),\partial\mathcal A).
\]

With \(h=1/N\), the current continuum candidate is
\[
h\log P_N
=
\frac1N\sum_{i=1}^{N}
\log\Phi\!\left(\frac{\Delta_i}{\sigma}\right)
\]
and the proposed limiting functional is
\[
\int_0^T
\log\Phi\!\left(\frac{\Delta(t)}{\sigma}\right)dt.
\]

## Certificate policy

Each future P/PN certificate must contain:

1. governing theorem/proposition;
2. definitions and hypotheses;
3. exact verification;
4. numerical or symbolic reproducibility;
5. limitation/boundary;
6. relation to the companion P or PN branch.

## Important status rule

The P and PN branches are intentionally developed in parallel. Neither branch is to be represented as complete merely because the other branch supplies a useful component.

The current continuum convergence is a candidate result pending a general proof under explicit assumptions.

**No certificate claims a universal theorem until its assumptions and verification are written explicitly.**
