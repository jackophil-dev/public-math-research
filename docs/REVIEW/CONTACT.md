1. **Academic Rigor:** Inquiries and feedback should focus on mathematical definitions, arithmetic constraints, computational steps, or logical consistency.
2. **Strict Boundary:** Proprietary developments, internal replicator systems, and non-public codebases are strictly isolated and will not be discussed via public or open channels.
3. **Secure Communication:** For sensitive or in-depth technical cryptographic/arithmetic proofs, encrypted communications can be arranged upon initial contact via email.
