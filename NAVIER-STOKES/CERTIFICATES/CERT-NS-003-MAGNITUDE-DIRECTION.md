# CERT-NS-003 — Vorticity Magnitude/Direction Factorization

## Objective

Separate vorticity magnitude from direction on the region where vorticity is nonzero.

## Definition

\[
\rho=|\omega|,
\qquad
\omega=\rho\xi,
\qquad
|\xi|=1,
\qquad
\rho>0.
\]

## Result

The factorization is kinematic and exact on the region \(\rho>0\).

## Status

**PROVED** on \(\rho>0\).

## Limitation

The zero-vorticity set requires separate treatment. No continuation through \(\rho=0\) is asserted by this certificate.
