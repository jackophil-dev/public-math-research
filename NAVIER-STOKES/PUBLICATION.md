## Scope and boundary

The public track records local vorticity structure, enstrophy-related identities, geometric dissipation, and the SPN structural identity, together with assumptions and open obligations. It does **not** claim a completed proof of global regularity for the three-dimensional incompressible Navier–Stokes equations or a finite-time singularity construction.
