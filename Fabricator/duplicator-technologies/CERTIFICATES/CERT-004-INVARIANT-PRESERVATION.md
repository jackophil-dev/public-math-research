# CERT-004 — Invariant Preservation

## Objective

Declare the invariants that every permitted transformation must preserve.

## Specification

For an invariant \(I\),

\[
I(T(s))=I(s)
\]

must hold for every transition \(T\) in the declared admissible transformation class.

Energy or conservation statements require their own physical hypotheses and balance laws.
