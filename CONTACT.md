## Collaboration and paid work

For inquiries, please email a short description of the goal, expected deliverable, deadline, and available budget. We can discuss scope, feasibility, price, and delivery terms before work begins.

Potential areas for discussion include:

- **Research organization and technical writing:** structuring research notes, theorem registers, certificate documentation, and technical reports.
- **Reproducible computational work:** small Python scripts, exact-arithmetic checks, and documented computational experiments when the inputs and verification criteria are specified.
- **Code and repository documentation:** README improvements, project organization, issue triage, and clear setup or usage instructions.
- **Technical review support:** checking stated calculations, assumptions, consistency, and reproducibility within an agreed scope.

These are areas for potential collaboration, not a claim that every project or result is already validated. Mathematical conclusions remain subject to their stated hypotheses and the evidence supplied. Open problems are not advertised as solved.

## Payment and support

Payment methods and terms are agreed directly with the client before a paid engagement begins. **No donation, sponsorship, or checkout link is currently advertised here.** Please email first to discuss a specific request.

GitHub repositories and public research records document work; they do not by themselves establish a client contract, guarantee a result, or process a payment.

## Research integrity

Public research distinguishes **PROVED**, **DERIVED**, **COMPUTATIONALLY VERIFIED**, and **OPEN** results where appropriate. A request for paid work does not change the evidence standard or the status of an unresolved claim.
