# BSD — Theorems and Proofs

## Proven structural theorem
The Mordell–Weil theorem states that \(E(\mathbb Q)\) is finitely generated, hence \(E(\mathbb Q)\cong\mathbb Z^r\oplus T\) for a finite torsion subgroup \(T\).

## Conjectural statements
BSD rank equality \(r=\operatorname{ord}_{s=1}L(E,s)\) and the refined leading-term formula are conjectures in general. They must not be labelled **PROVED** for arbitrary elliptic curves.

## Geometric integral identity
For \(r(\theta)=r_0e^{b\theta}\), the arc-length element is
\[
ds=\sqrt{r^2+(dr/d\theta)^2}\,d\theta
=r_0e^{b\theta}\sqrt{1+b^2}\,d\theta.
\]
Integration over an interval of angular width \(\Delta\theta\) gives the recorded expression for \(b\ne0\). This proves a geometric formula under its parameter assumptions, not a correspondence to an elliptic-curve period.

Curve-specific arithmetic and any bridge to \(\Omega_E\) remain open until exact input data and a map are supplied.
