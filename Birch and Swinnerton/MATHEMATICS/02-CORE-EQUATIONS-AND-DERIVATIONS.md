# BSD — Core Equations and Derivations

## Analytic rank
\[
r_{an}=\operatorname{ord}_{s=1}L(E,s).
\]
This is the multiplicity of the zero at \(s=1\), within the analytic formulation of the L-function.

## Conjectural leading term
Under a fixed normalization, BSD predicts
\[
\frac{L^{(r)}(E,1)}{r!}
=\Omega_E\operatorname{Reg}(E)|\Sha(E/\mathbb Q)|
\frac{\prod_{p\mid N}c_p}{|E(\mathbb Q)_{tors}|^2},
\]
with \(r\) the algebraic rank if rank equality holds. Period conventions must match the chosen L-function normalization.

## Geometric arc-length identity
The public certificate records
\[
I=\frac{r_0\sqrt{1+b^2}}{b}(e^{b\Delta\theta}-1),\quad b\ne0,
\]
for a logarithmic-spiral arc-length model. This is a geometric integral, not automatically the arithmetic period \(\Omega_E\). Identifying it with an elliptic-curve period requires a specified curve, differential, cycle, and proven correspondence or pullback.

No derivation here proves general BSD.
