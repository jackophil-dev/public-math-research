# BSD — Computational Verification

A curve-specific certificate must record the exact Weierstrass equation, conductor, minimal-model status, software/version, precision, and commands/outputs. Checks may include torsion, rank bounds, local Tamagawa factors, period normalization, regulator, and Taylor coefficients of \(L(E,s)\). Numerical approximations need precision and error control.

Check the BSD formula only with consistent normalization, distinguishing known factors from conjectural or uncomputed quantities, especially \(|\Sha|\).

The public record says the original curve/parameter input was not recovered. Therefore this folder does not fabricate a curve or claim curve-specific BSD verification. The spiral integral is a geometric calculation, not an arithmetic-period computation.
