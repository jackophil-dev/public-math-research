## Scope and publication note

The records address logarithmic-spiral kinematics, non-closure and unboundedness, shell-fit and residual testing, projection and viewpoint, variable-pitch dynamics, radial stability, and coupled radius–pitch dynamics.

All records listed here are part of the public Part I research archive. Historical “PRIVATE” labels retained in source documents are annotations from the working history and do not mean that the corresponding material has been withheld from this release. Open verification requirements remain explicitly open.

## Geometry-to-arithmetic boundary

For a logarithmic spiral, geometric identities can be derived under stated kinematic assumptions. Such identities do not, on their own, identify an elliptic curve, its L-function, real period, regulator, Mordell–Weil group, or Tate–Shafarevich group. A proposed bridge must define the objects and mapping and prove the relevant properties. The BSD connection remains open wherever that obligation has not been met.
