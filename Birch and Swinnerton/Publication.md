## Scope note

This is a research-in-progress preprint. It does not claim a proof of the general Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer Conjecture. The archive distinguishes proved statements under stated hypotheses, computationally verified results, derived identities, and open research obligations.

The supplementary Ammonite records document a geometric investigation; they are not additional proofs of BSD.

The GitHub registers in this directory are navigational summaries. The Zenodo deposit remains the canonical versioned archival record for the complete research files.
