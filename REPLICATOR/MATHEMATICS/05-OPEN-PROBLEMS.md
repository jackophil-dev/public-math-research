# Replicator — Open Problems and Proof Obligations

1. Define a concrete target, feedstock, sensors, actuators, state variables, transition dynamics, and admissible controls.
2. Establish observability and controllability for that model.
3. Prove stability/robustness with a valid Lyapunov or other argument, including noise, delay, and uncertainty.
4. Close material, energy, heat, waste, transport, assembly, and verification budgets with units and boundaries.
5. Verify software gates and test hardware failure modes independently.
6. Separate formal proofs, simulation results, and physical experiments.

Evidence summary: invariant-set induction is **PROVED CONDITIONALLY**; architecture is a **SPECIFICATION**; implementation-wide verification is **OPEN**; universal matter reconstruction and experimental performance are **NOT ESTABLISHED**.
