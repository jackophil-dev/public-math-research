# Theorem 002 — Lyapunov Stability/Convergence (Conditional)

Suppose a positive-definite Lyapunov function $V$ is defined on a specified domain and the system satisfies the regularity and invariance hypotheses of an applicable Lyapunov theorem. If $\dot V\le-\alpha\|e(t)\|^2$, with $\alpha>0$, then a stability or convergence conclusion may follow under the remaining hypotheses of that theorem.

## Proof boundary

The displayed inequality alone does not identify the dynamics, prove forward invariance, establish existence for all time, or determine a convergence rate. The exact theorem must match the domain, regularity, error definition, and bounds.
